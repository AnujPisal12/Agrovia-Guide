Getting Started

Who are you on Agrovia?

Agrovia connects four key stakeholders in one centralized digital ecosystem. Choose your role below to jump to your guide.

🌾

Farmer

Deliver your produce to the Agrovia warehouse. The admin registers your harvest and you get paid once quality testing is complete.
🏭

Register incoming harvests, generate QR batch IDs, run quality grading, manage inventory, and fulfill retailer orders.
🏪

Log into the POS system, receive dispatched stock, and sell to end consumers.
🛒

Consumer

Buy fresh produce at Agrovia kiosks and scan QR codes to verify origin & freshness.

Role 1

Farmer — How It Works

Farmers don't have a separate dashboard login. The Warehouse Admin handles all digital registration on your behalf when you arrive at the facility.

ℹ️

No App or Login Needed for Farmers

Farmers simply show up at the Agrovia warehouse with their produce. The warehouse admin registers the harvest, runs quality grading, and the system automatically calculates your payout — fully transparent, no intermediaries.

1

Register with Agrovia (One-Time)

First-time farmers provide their name, farm location, and bank details to the warehouse admin. The admin registers them in the system. Your name will appear in the "Select registered farmer" dropdown for all future transactions.

💡 This only needs to be done once — after registration you're in the system permanently.
2

Deliver Your Produce to the Warehouse

Bring your harvested produce in crates to your nearest Agrovia warehouse (Mumbai, Pune, or Satara). The warehouse admin will take over and handle the digital intake on the platform.

3

Wait for Quality Grading

Your produce is quality-tested by the admin. The grade (A, B, or C) determines the price — higher grade means higher payout. The system calculates your Farmer Payout automatically after grading.

📈 Grade A produce earns the highest payout rate; the pricing structure is fully transparent and visible on the platform.
4

Receive Direct Payment

Once grading is confirmed, your payout is processed directly to your registered bank account — no middlemen, no deductions. The exact Rs./kg rate is tied to the quality grade assigned to your batch.

5

Log Out

The Farmer role does not require a personal login — the warehouse admin handles data entry on your behalf. However, if a warehouse admin finishes a session on a shared device, they should click the profile icon in the top-right corner and select Logout to protect your farm records and payment data.

🔒 Always ensure the operations staff logs out of shared devices after registering your delivery.

Role 2

Operations & Warehouse Admin Guide

As the admin, you are the hub of the entire supply chain — you register every harvest, grade every batch, and manage all inventory from intake to dispatch.

🔑

Login to the Operations Dashboard

Go to agroviaa.vercel.app, select Operations Management, and enter the pass-key to unlock the full admin panel.

Pass-Key
PB2806
Section A

Product Management → Farmer Intake

1

Open Farmer Intake

From the left sidebar, click Product Management, then select the Farmer Intake tab. This opens the New Harvest Registration form.

2

Fill in the Harvest Registration Form

Complete all fields in the form:

Product Selection — Choose the crop type from the dropdown (e.g. Cauliflower, Tomato, Lettuce)
Farmer Details — Select the registered farmer from the dropdown. Use the + button to register a new farmer on the spot if needed.
Harvest Date — Enter the actual harvest date (required field, marked with *)
Target Warehouse — Select the warehouse where this batch will be stored (Mumbai, Pune, or Satara)
Quantity (kg) — Enter the total weight of the produce delivered
Storage Method — Choose Normal Storage or Cold Storage from the dropdown
3

Submit — Batch Created!

Click Submit / Register Harvest. The system instantly generates a unique Batch ID (e.g. BTH-MMM8ANF9-L27E) and a QR code for the batch. The batch status is set to Test Pending.

🏷️ Print and attach the QR code label to the physical crates immediately after this step.
4

Proceed to Quality Testing

On the batch confirmation screen, click "Proceed to Quality Testing". This takes you directly to the Quality Reports section with the new batch pre-loaded.

Section B

Quality Reports

5

Open Quality Reports & Select a Batch

Click Quality Reports in the left sidebar. All batches with Test Pending status will appear in the list. Select the batch you want to test — you can search by Batch ID or farmer name.

6

Section C

Warehouse Inventory

Click Warehouse Inventory in the sidebar to get a live overview of all stock across your three warehouses — Mumbai, Pune, and Satara.

8

Current Inventory Tab

The default view shows all batches in stock, filterable by Fresh, Consume Soon, and Expired. Each batch entry shows produce name, Batch ID, farmer, quantity (kg), shelf-life status, and grade. Use the search bar to look up specific batches.

To look up any crate, type or scan the Batch ID into the Scan Barcode / QR Code field, or click Open Camera to scan a physical QR label with your device camera. The full tracking details and AI suggestions appear on the right panel.

📦 The top stat bar shows: Total Batches · Fresh Stock · Low Shelf-Life · Total Crates across all warehouses.
9

Live Logistics Tab

Switch to the Live Logistics tab to monitor the real-time delivery status of all dispatched batches. Track which batches are in transit, which have been delivered, and any delivery exceptions.

10

Crate Management Tab

Use the Crate Management tab to track the physical crates in circulation — which are at the warehouse, which are with retailers, and which need to be collected and returned.

11

Orders Tab — Fulfill Retailer Orders

When a retailer places a restocking request, it appears here under the Orders tab. Review the order, select the appropriate batches from inventory to fulfill it, and confirm dispatch. The retailer's kiosk inventory updates automatically once dispatch is confirmed.

🏪 Orders are tied to specific warehouse locations — Pune kiosks are fulfilled from Pune Warehouse stock, etc.
12

Log Out of the Operations Dashboard

When your shift or session is complete, click the circular avatar / profile icon in the top-right corner of the dashboard and select Logout. This closes your authenticated session and protects sensitive operational and payment data.

🔒 Always log out when stepping away from a shared terminal — the dashboard contains farmer payment records and full inventory data.

Role 3

Retailer / Kiosk Guide

Manage your Agrovia retail kiosk — from accepting stock to billing customers through the built-in POS. Each kiosk has its own unique pass-key and location.

🔑

Sign In to Your Kiosk Portal

Go to agroviaa.vercel.app, select Retailer, and enter your kiosk's unique pass-key. At launch there are 3 warehouses (Mumbai, Pune, Satara) and multiple kiosks — each with their own credentials. After login you land on the Retailer Portal dashboard showing Live Inventory and Real-time Pricing.

Demo Pass-Key
PB2806
Tab A

Customer Billing (POS)

1

Open Customer Billing (POS)

From the Retailer Portal header, click the Customer Billing (POS) tab. The POS screen has two panels: the left for entering customer & item details, and the right for the live Current Bill.

2

Enter Customer Details

In the Customer Details section, type the customer's phone number (e.g. 9730883800) into the Customer Phone field.

✅ Existing Member
The member's name and ID (e.g. AGV-883800) auto-appear with a PREMIUM badge. Members get automatic discounts applied to their Total to Pay.
🆕 New Customer
Click Create Member ID to register them on the spot. They'll get a unique Agrovia Member ID for future discounts.
3

Add Items to Cart

In the Add Items section, use the dropdown to Scan QR or Select from your inventory — choose the produce batch available in your kiosk stock. Alternatively, type the Batch ID directly into the text field below. Then enter the Quantity (kg) and click the blue + Add to Cart button.

📦 Items shown as "Out of Stock" (e.g. Potato, Onion, Tomato) need to be restocked first via Inventory Procurement — see Tab B below.
4

Review the Current Bill (Right Panel)

The right-side panel shows a live Current Bill with each item, its Batch ID, weight, and price. For members, the discounted Total to Pay is displayed prominently in blue. Select the Payment Method (Cash or other options) from the dropdown.

5

Checkout & Note the Unique Bill Code

Click the Checkout button. A bill is generated with a unique code and a QR code. Note down the unique code or hand the bill to the customer. The customer can use this code later in the Consumer portal to verify their purchase and trace produce origin.

🔍 Ask the customer to zoom in on the QR code on the bill for best scanning results with their phone camera.
6

Log Out

When done, click the circular avatar in the top-right corner of the screen and select Logout from the dropdown.

Tab B

Inventory Procurement

If items are out of stock at your kiosk, use this tab to place a restocking order directly from a warehouse. Click Inventory Procurement in the portal header.

1

Place a Warehouse Order

Under Place Warehouse Order, use the Select Warehouse dropdown to choose your supply warehouse (e.g. Pune Warehouse). Then pick the Product Batch from available stock and enter the Quantity in crates.

2

Choose a Payment Option

Select your preferred payment method from the Payment Option dropdown — either Pay Instantly (immediate payment on order) or Pay Later (credit arrangement). Then click Submit Order.

3

Track Your Order in Recent Orders

The right panel shows a Recent Orders list. Each order shows its Order ID (e.g. ORD-MLHLP0B6-VFN8), number of crates, source batch, warehouse, and payment status. Orders are fulfilled by the Operations team from the warehouse side and will update to Fulfilled once dispatched.

🏭 The Operations team must fulfill your order from their dashboard — stock will appear in your kiosk inventory once dispatched.

1

Verify Your Purchase — Customer Tab

On the Customer tab you'll see a Verify Your Purchase card. Take the unique bill code given to you at the kiosk counter after checkout, enter it into the Enter bill code field, and click the search icon. Your purchase details and product quality information will appear instantly.

🧾 Find your 8-character code at the bottom of your receipt, near the barcode — look for it printed clearly under the QR.
2

Scan the Bill QR — Scan QR Code Button

Instead of typing the code, tap the Scan QR Code button (top-right of the Customer tab). Point your camera at the QR code printed on your receipt — zoom in for best results. The system will auto-fill and verify the code for you.

🔍 You can also use the Scan Product option from the left sidebar as an alternative entry point for scanning.
3

Trace Your Produce — Traceability Tab

Click the Traceability tab (or select Traceability from the left navbar). Enter a Batch ID (found on your receipt or the product label, e.g. BTH-MMM8ANF9-L27E). The platform shows you the complete journey of that produce — farmer origin, harvest date, quality grade, warehouse entry, and kiosk delivery.

4

Join as a Member — Membership Tab

Click Membership in the left sidebar to view available membership plans. Select a plan and register with your phone number to get your unique Agrovia Member ID. Once enrolled, you'll automatically receive discounted pricing at any Agrovia kiosk POS — just give the kiosk operator your phone number at billing.

5

View All Members — Members Tab

The Members tab lets you look up registered Agrovia members. Kiosk operators and customers can confirm membership status and details here.

6

Get Help — Built-In Chatbot

See the chat bubble icon in the bottom-right corner of the portal? That's Agrovia's rule-based support chatbot. Click it to get instant answers to common questions — about membership, traceability, kiosk locations, billing queries, and more. No waiting, no phone calls.

🤖 The chatbot is rule-based and designed to handle the most frequent customer queries quickly and accurately.
7

Log Out of the Consumer Portal

Once you're done verifying your purchase or managing your membership, click the profile / avatar icon in the top-right corner of the portal and select Logout. This is especially important if you're using a shared kiosk terminal or a public device.

🔒 Logging out protects your Agrovia Member ID and purchase history from being accessed by others.
